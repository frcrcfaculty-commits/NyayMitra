# Tenant Rights in India

## Your Rights as a Tenant

As a tenant in India, you have several legal protections under various state Rent Control Acts and the Model Tenancy Act, 2021.

### Security Deposit

- **Maximum deposit**: Under the Model Tenancy Act, 2021, the security deposit for residential premises cannot exceed **2 months' rent**
- **Refund timeline**: The landlord must refund the security deposit within **1 month** of vacating the property, after deducting any legitimate dues
- **Deductions**: Only legitimate damages (beyond normal wear and tear) can be deducted. The landlord must provide an itemized list of deductions

### Rent Increase

- Your landlord **cannot increase rent arbitrarily** during the tenancy period
- Any rent increase must follow the terms specified in the rent agreement
- Under most state laws, rent can only be increased by **10% per year** (varies by state)

### Eviction Protection

You cannot be evicted without proper legal process:

1. The landlord must serve a written notice (usually 1-3 months depending on state law)
2. Eviction can only be for valid reasons:
   - Non-payment of rent
   - Misuse of property
   - Landlord's genuine need for personal use
3. Only a court can order eviction — **self-help eviction is illegal**

### Essential Services

Your landlord **cannot cut off**:
- Water supply
- Electricity
- Gas connection
- Any other essential service

Cutting off essential services to force eviction is a criminal offense.

## What To Do If Your Rights Are Violated

1. **Document everything**: Keep copies of rent agreement, receipts, and communications
2. **Send a legal notice**: Through a registered advocate
3. **Approach the Rent Authority**: File a complaint with the local Rent Controller
4. **File a police complaint**: If essential services are cut off
5. **Contact Legal Aid**: Call NALSA helpline **15100** for free legal aid

## Relevant Laws

- Model Tenancy Act, 2021
- State-specific Rent Control Acts
- Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Section 108)
- Indian Contract Act, 1872
