# Filing a Consumer Complaint

## Your Rights as a Consumer

The **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** gives you comprehensive rights:

1. **Right to Safety** — Protection against hazardous goods
2. **Right to Information** — Complete details about products/services
3. **Right to Choose** — Access to variety of goods at competitive prices
4. **Right to be Heard** — Your complaints must be heard
5. **Right to Redressal** — Fair settlement of genuine grievances
6. **Right to Consumer Education** — Knowledge about consumer rights

## When Can You File a Complaint?

You can file a consumer complaint for:

- **Defective products** — Products that don't meet quality standards
- **Deficient services** — Services that fall short of what was promised
- **Unfair trade practices** — Misleading advertisements, hidden charges
- **Overcharging** — Charging more than MRP or agreed price
- **Restrictive trade practices** — Forcing you to buy unwanted products/services

## How to File a Complaint

### Option 1: Online (Recommended)
1. Visit the **National Consumer Helpline**: [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in)
2. Or use the **INGRAM portal**: [edaakhil.nic.in](https://edaakhil.nic.in) for e-filing
3. Register and file your complaint with supporting documents
4. Track your complaint status online

### Option 2: Helpline
- Call **1800-11-4000** (toll-free) or **14404**
- Available Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM

### Option 3: In Person
1. Visit your **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum**
2. File a written complaint with:
   - Your details (name, address)
   - Opposite party details
   - Description of complaint
   - Relief sought
   - Supporting documents (bills, receipts, warranty cards)

## Jurisdiction

| Forum | Claim Amount |
|-------|-------------|
| District Forum | Up to ₹1 crore |
| State Commission | ₹1 crore to ₹10 crore |
| National Commission | Above ₹10 crore |

## Important Points

- **No lawyer needed**: You can represent yourself
- **Nominal fee**: Filing fees are minimal (₹100 to ₹5,000 depending on claim)
- **Time limit**: File within **2 years** of the cause of action
- **E-commerce**: Online purchases are also covered
- **No court fee for senior citizens**: Many forums waive fees for senior citizens

## Relevant Laws

- Consumer Protection Act, 2019
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
- Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (for MRP violations)
- Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (for food-related complaints)
