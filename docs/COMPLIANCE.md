# NyayMitra — Compliance & Legal Guidelines

## Disclaimer Requirements
Every screen of the application MUST display the following disclaimer:

> **⚠️ NyayMitra is an educational legal awareness tool, NOT a law firm or
> legal advisor. The information provided is for general awareness only and
> does not constitute legal advice. Always consult a qualified advocate for
> your specific situation.**

This disclaimer must appear:
- In the footer of every page
- Before any AI-generated response
- In the app's About/FAQ section
- In the app store listing (when published)

## Prohibited Claims
- ❌ Never use the phrase "AI lawyer" or "robot lawyer"
- ❌ Never claim the AI can "represent" anyone
- ❌ Never claim responses are "legal advice"
- ❌ Never claim responses are guaranteed accurate
- ✅ Use: "legal awareness", "educational tool", "general information"

## Citation Integrity
- All statute references must link to official sources (Indian Kanoon, etc.)
- AI-generated citations MUST show a ⚠️ "unverified" badge until confirmed
- Verified citations show a ✅ badge with source link

## Data Privacy
- Follow Indian IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023 requirements
- No personal data stored without explicit consent
- Chat data encrypted at rest (Supabase handles this)
- Users can delete their data at any time

## Languages
- All legal terms should include the original Hindi/Sanskrit term where applicable
- Translations must be reviewed by native speakers before production use
